What exactly is reverse recruiting?

Reverse recruiting is a service where professional recruiters apply to jobs on your behalf. Instead of you spending hours searching and applying to positions, our team handles the entire job search process - from finding suitable opportunities to submitting tailored applications.

Our approach flips the traditional job search model. While traditional recruiting helps companies find candidates, reverse recruiting helps candidates find and secure positions that match their skills, experience, and career goals.

Who can benefit from reverse recruiting services?

Reverse recruiting is beneficial for professionals at all career levels:

  • Junior Professionals: Those early in their careers who need help standing out in a competitive job market
  • Mid-Career Professionals: Experienced individuals looking to advance their careers or transition to new industries
  • Senior Executives: High-level professionals seeking discreet, targeted job searches
  • International Candidates: Those looking for opportunities that offer visa sponsorship or relocation support

Essentially, anyone who wants to save time and increase their chances of landing interviews can benefit from our services.

How does the reverse recruiting process work?

Our process is straightforward and designed to maximize your chances of success:

  1. Initial Consultation: We start with a comprehensive consultation to understand your career goals, skills, experience, and preferences.
  2. Resume Optimization: Our experts craft or optimize your resume to highlight your strengths and ensure it passes through ATS systems.
  3. Targeted Job Search: We search for positions that match your profile and career goals.
  4. Personalized Applications: We submit tailored applications for each position, customizing cover letters and responses.
  5. Tracking & Reporting: You receive real-time updates through our shared dashboard and weekly progress reports.
What are your pricing options?

We offer flexible pricing options to meet different needs:

  • 1-Month Plan: $140/month for full service including resume optimization, job search, and applications.
  • 3-Month Plan: $400/3 months (save $40) for extended service with priority support.
  • Custom Plan: $1.40 per application for those who want to control the number of applications.

There are no hidden fees or placement fees. The price you see is the price you pay.

What kind of results can I expect?

While we cannot guarantee specific job offers (as the final decision always rests with employers), our clients typically see:

  • A 95% interview rate for applications submitted
  • 3x faster placement compared to traditional job searching
  • Significant time savings (average 15-20 hours per week)
  • Access to opportunities that may not be publicly advertised

Our success is measured by the number of interviews our clients secure and the quality of opportunities they're presented with.

How many jobs will you apply to on my behalf?

The number of applications depends on the plan you choose:

  • 1-Month Plan: Up to 50 applications per month
  • 3-Month Plan: Up to 150 applications over the three-month period
  • Custom Plan: You determine the number of applications based on your needs

We focus on quality over quantity, ensuring each application is tailored to the specific position and company.

Do you work with specific industries or job types?

We work across a wide range of industries and job types, including but not limited to:

  • Technology and IT
  • Finance and Banking
  • Healthcare
  • Marketing and Sales
  • Human Resources
  • Operations and Management
  • Engineering
  • Consulting

Our team has experience in multiple sectors and can adapt our approach to different industries and job levels.

Do you charge a placement fee or commission?

No, we do not charge any placement fees or commissions. Our pricing is transparent and fixed:

  • No percentage of your salary
  • No commission on job offers
  • No hidden fees or additional charges

This approach ensures our interests are perfectly aligned with yours - we succeed when you succeed, not when you accept a lower offer.

How long does it typically take to see results?

Timeline can vary based on factors like your industry, experience level, and job market conditions, but typically:

  • First applications are submitted within 3-5 business days of onboarding
  • Most clients start receiving interview calls within 2-3 weeks
  • Job offers typically come within 4-8 weeks of starting our service

On average, our clients secure positions 3x faster than those conducting traditional job searches.

How do you track applications and report progress?

We provide complete transparency through our tracking system:

  • Shared Google Sheets Dashboard: Real-time tracking of all applications submitted, responses received, and interview schedules
  • Weekly Progress Reports: Detailed summaries of activities, responses, and next steps
  • Direct Communication: Regular updates via email or phone as preferred

You'll always know exactly where you stand in your job search process.
